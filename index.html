About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Graphs2App mobile app (“the App”) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The App is provided by bijencreations (“we”, “us”, “our”).

The App lets you upload a performance graph image, calibrate it, define data points and formula functions, enter values such as weight, pressure altitude and temperature, and generate a performance card and table that you can export as a PDF. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account or sign in, we collect your email address and the sign-in details needed to authenticate you. Authentication is handled by our backend provider, InsForge.

Content you create or upload

To provide the App’s features, the following content may be stored in our backend storage and linked to your account:

  • Graph images you upload
  • Calibration points, data points and saved graph functions, including the graph IDs you choose
  • Math formula functions you create
  • Input values you enter (such as weight, pressure altitude and temperature) and the performance cards, tables and PDFs generated from them, where the App saves them

Please do not upload content that contains personal information about other people.

Device permissions

The App may ask for access to your photos or files so you can pick a graph image, and to save or share a PDF. We only access files you choose. The App does not run in the background to read your files.

Technical data

Like most online services, our backend provider may process basic technical data, such as IP address, device and request logs, to keep the service running and secure.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS or call logs, microphone or camera recordings, advertising ID or payment information.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in
  • To store and show your graphs, functions, formulas and performance results
  • To generate performance cards, tables and PDF exports that you request
  • To keep the App secure, fix problems and prevent misuse
  • To reply to your questions and requests
  • To meet legal obligations

Artificial intelligence

The App does not use artificial intelligence or machine learning features. Your uploaded images, formulas and data are not used to train AI models.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only as described here:

Who What they do Data involved
InsForge Backend provider for authentication and file/data storage Account details and the content you save in the App
Legal or safety requests Disclosure when required by law or to protect rights and safety Only what is legally required

InsForge processes data on our behalf to provide these services. Google Play handles downloads and updates of the App under Google’s own privacy policy.

Storage, security and transfers

Data is sent between the App and our backend over encrypted connections, and access to stored data is limited to your account and authorised administrators. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our backend provider may store and process data on servers in countries other than your own. By using the App you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

How long we keep data

We keep your account information and saved content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or ask us to delete your data, we remove it from our active systems within 30 days, except where we must keep certain records to meet legal obligations. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.

Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and all data linked to it, including uploaded graph images, saved graph functions, formulas and results.

  1. Email bijencreations@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up, with the subject “Delete my account”.
  2. We may ask you to confirm it is you.
  3. We delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you only want specific items removed (for example one graph), tell us in the same email.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the App or by email before it takes effect.
