1. Information handled by the app

Account and sign-in information

Graphs2App uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, Google and Firebase may process account-related information such as your Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile image, depending on the permissions and configuration used by the app. We use sign-in to authenticate users and associate app data with an account where applicable.

Graphs, formulas and app content

Information you enter or create in Graphs2App—such as graphs, formulas, functions, program entries, settings or other saved content—may be stored locally on your device and, where cloud-sync or backend features are enabled, on the app’s cloud service. The exact categories of cloud-stored content depend on the features you use and must be verified against the released app.

Technical and diagnostic information

The app and its service providers may process technical information needed for authentication, security, service operation and troubleshooting. Whether the app collects analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, device identifiers or diagnostic logs depends on the SDKs and configuration included in the release. Verify these items before publication and update this policy if they apply.

AI services

Based on the current product information provided by the publisher, Graphs2App does not send user data to Gemini or another AI service. If an AI feature or third-party AI integration is added later, this policy and the app’s disclosures must be updated before that feature is used.

2. How information is used

Information is used only as needed for the app’s functions and operation, including:

  • Signing users in and maintaining authentication sessions.
  • Saving, retrieving or synchronising app content when those features are used.
  • Providing app functionality and responding to support requests.
  • Protecting the service, investigating misuse and troubleshooting technical issues.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

We do not claim to use information for advertising or analytics unless those features are actually included and disclosed in the released app.

3. Sharing and service providers

Graphs2App uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. Google may process authentication information to provide these services under its own terms and privacy policy. If Graphs2App stores or synchronises content through a cloud backend, the provider of that backend may process the information required to deliver that functionality.

Publisher must verify before release: identify the actual cloud/backend provider and any enabled Firebase services, analytics, crash-reporting, hosting, backup or other SDKs. List the relevant providers and data sharing here. Do not publish this policy until this section accurately reflects the production build.

We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of users or the service.

4. Storage and security

Some app content may remain on your device. Other information may be stored by Firebase Authentication and, if enabled, the app’s cloud/backend service. The precise storage location and data categories depend on the services configured in the app.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, and rely on the security controls of the services we use. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The publisher should verify that network traffic uses secure connections and that cloud databases and storage are protected by appropriate access rules.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed to provide the app’s functions, meet legitimate operational needs and comply with applicable legal requirements. Locally stored information may remain on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app, subject to the app’s actual storage behaviour. Removing the app may not delete information already stored in a cloud account.

To request deletion of your account and associated cloud data, contact bijencreations@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and include “Graphs2App account and data deletion” in the subject. Do not send your password.

Required implementation check: the publisher must ensure that account deletion requests are actually fulfilled, including deletion of associated data held by the app’s backend and relevant service providers, subject to lawful retention obligations. Google Play may require a clear in-app deletion path and an external web resource for requesting account deletion when users can create accounts. An email link in this policy alone may not satisfy every applicable requirement; publish a dedicated account-deletion request webpage and implement the in-app flow before release if required.

6. Children’s privacy

Graphs2App is not described here as being designed specifically for children. The publisher must set the correct target audience in Google Play Console and comply with applicable children’s privacy and Families policies if children are among the intended audience. If the app is directed to children or knowingly collects children’s personal information, this section and the app’s data practices must be reviewed and updated accordingly.

7. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to sign in where sign-in is optional. You may contact us to ask about the information associated with your account or to request deletion. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law and our ability to verify the request.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with its effective date updated. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate method where required.
